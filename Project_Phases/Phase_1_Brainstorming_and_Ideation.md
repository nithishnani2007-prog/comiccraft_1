# Phase 1: Brainstorming & Ideation


## Problem Statement
Managing daily and event-based expenses manually is tedious and lacks actionable budget insights.

## Ideation & Solution
PocketSmart AI automates budget tracking by using Google Gemini 1.5 multimodal AI to scan receipts, parse costs, and provide budget-conscious recommendations.

## Target Audience
Students, homemakers, and small teams needing fast, frictionless expense breakdowns.
